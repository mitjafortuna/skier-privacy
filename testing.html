Snowlore — instructions for testers

Thank you for helping. This should take about ten minutes to set up and then however long you feel like skiing.

What you need

  • An Android phone (Android 7 or newer)
  • The Google account you actually use on that phone — the address you give me
  • has to be that one, or the game will never appear for you

Getting the game

1. Open this link on the phone and press Become a tester: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/si.fortuna.snowlore

Nothing installs yet. This step tells Google you are allowed to see the app, and it will not appear in the Play Store until you have done it.

2. Then install Snowlore as normal from https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=si.fortuna.snowlore

If the Store says the app is not available, wait ten minutes and try again — there is a delay between opting in and the listing appearing, and it catches almost everybody.

3. Please stay opted in. Google requires twelve testers to remain in the programme for fourteen consecutive days before the game can be released publicly. Leaving early sets that back, so if you want to stop, telling me is more useful than quietly leaving.

What I would like you to do

Play it a few times over the fortnight rather than once for an hour. Things that only show up on the second or third session are exactly the ones I cannot find myself.

Beyond that, no script. But if you want direction:

  • Play Adventure from the first course and see how far you get
  • Play Endless at least once and keep going past a minute, where it starts
  • getting harder and something comes after you
  • Look in Settings — try tilt and touch controls, and change the language if
  • your phone is not in English

What is worth telling me

Anything that felt wrong, even if you cannot say why. "The third course felt unfair" is more useful than a bug report with no feeling attached, because I can find the cause and you cannot be wrong about how it felt.

Specifically useful:

  • Anything that crashed, froze, or vanished
  • Anywhere the controls did not do what you expected
  • Text that is cut off, overlapping, or in the wrong language
  • Anything that looked broken or unfinished
  • The game draining battery or getting the phone hot
  • Places you got stuck or confused about what to do

Not useful to hold back: if it is boring, say it is boring. That is the most expensive thing to find out after release.

How to report

Easiest is the feedback button inside the game — Settings screen → About → Send feedback. It opens your email app with the game version and your phone model already filled in, which saves me guessing. You can edit or delete anything in it before sending.

Otherwise just message me.

A screenshot is worth a paragraph. On most Android phones: power + volume down together.

What the game does with your data

Nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and does not go online while you play. Your progress and settings are saved on your phone and nowhere else. The full policy is at https://mitjafortuna.github.io/skier-privacy/privacy.html.

The one exception is the feedback button, which opens *your* email app with a message *you* choose to send — nothing goes anywhere unless you send it.

If something goes badly wrong

Uninstalling and reinstalling from the same Play Store link is safe, and clears any corrupt save. You will lose your progress and best times, which for a test build is not much of a loss.

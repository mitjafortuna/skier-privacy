Privacy Policy for Skier

Effective date: TBD at first release.

The short version

Skier collects nothing about you. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What is stored

The game saves a single settings and progress file on your own device. It contains:

  • your best times, best score and furthest distance,
  • the stars you have earned and which courses you have unlocked,
  • your control, sound, vibration and language preferences.

This file never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back it up to. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is not collected

No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind. No location. No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. No advertising identifier. No device fingerprint. No crash or usage analytics. No cookies.

Permissions

The game requests vibration, used only for the crash and landing feedback you can switch off in Settings. It requests access to the motion sensors already present in your device, used only to read how you are tilting it in order to steer. Sensor readings are used in the moment and never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Skier does not request network access for gameplay. The only time it reaches the internet is when you deliberately tap a link — the donation link or this policy — which opens in your own browser, outside the game.

Children

Skier is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Feedback

The About screen has a button that opens your own email app with a message addressed to the developer. To make a report useful it arrives with three things already written in it: the game's version, your device model, and your operating system version.

Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and you can edit or delete any part of it — including those three lines — before you do. The game does not read your address book, does not know your email address, and sends nothing on its own. If you never tap the button, none of this happens.

Donations

The support link opens PayPal in your browser. Any payment happens entirely within PayPal and is covered by PayPal's own privacy policy. The game receives no information about it — not whether you donated, not how much, not who you are. Donating unlocks nothing in the game.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes what it collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change with it.
